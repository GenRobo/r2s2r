Approach

Three kinds of validity,
checked in order.

Image-to-3D pipelines pass their errors along unchecked, generated assets lack articulation and contact, and even a plausible simulator can make a pretrained policy act differently than it does on the real robot. Before simulation can improve a deployed policy, it has to reproduce that policy’s behavior.

Results / Policy-guided alignment

Valid geometry,
wrong behavior.

Our first simulator passed every geometric and physical check, yet the frozen policy started manipulating in only half of the simulated observations, against all of the real ones. Using the policy as a discriminator, we corrected camera appearance, viewpoint, embodiment and state feedback.

Task B / Plug insertion · π0.5 checkpoint 14999

Gated lateral corrections

A gate decides when to correct; a small head predicts a sideways fingertip shift of at most 20 mm, applied to π0.5’s action chunk during insertion. Trained on 80 simulated oracle episodes; no object pose reaches the head.

Top: one real trial per condition, real time. Bottom: simulation, one matched start, 3× speed.

Training and success definition
Gated head
Also predicts a gate; the shift applies only when the gate fires and the gripper reading is 0.5–0.6. 941 training decisions.
Gateless head
Applies every step; trained with zero targets outside insertion, so it stays near 0 mm elsewhere (median 0.15 mm). 2,241 training decisions.
Labels
A privileged oracle ran 80 episodes (seeds 100–179, disjoint from test seeds 0–19) and labelled the seat-minus-plug offset during insertion only.

The completed combined-feature corrector uses 853 training and 88 validation decisions selected from an 80-episode oracle collection. It minimizes supervised residual/gating losses, not an RL objective. Earlier experiments used DAgger; those data should not be assumed to train this specific head.

The current score checks blade-tip XY error below 6 mm and depth above 10 mm. It does not explicitly enforce orientation, maximum penetration, dwell, release stability or electrical contact. Active and interrupted experiments are excluded from the completed table.

Saved matching starts do not establish identical simulator revisions or diffusion-noise streams. A final comparison requires frozen identities, independently generated reset manifests and a validated physical success criterion.
